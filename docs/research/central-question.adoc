= Central question and research programme
:toc:

== Proposed central question

*How can people make, justify and revisit decisions about risk and opportunity
as real work, organisational pressures, controls and evidence change, while
preserving human agency, wellbeing and the capacity to learn?*

This is a design proposal, not a validated research result. The unit of analysis
is a situated work system: people pursuing goals with technology, resources,
constraints and competing demands. The unit of accountability is an explicit
decision with evidence, uncertainty, authority, conditions and review triggers.
The bowtie is the first projection of that model; it cannot represent the entire
system by itself. Support successful adaptations and opportunities alongside harm.

== Research foundations and concrete consequences

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Foundation | Source-grounded idea | Proposed application consequence
| Jens Rasmussen
| Risk management spans interacting organisational and societal levels, with
adaptation shaped by constraints and performance boundaries.
| Add actors, organisational levels, competing pressures, feedback delays,
decision boundaries and longitudinal drift. Preserve alternative explanations.
| James Reason
| The system approach examines conditions and defences surrounding fallible people.
| Model barrier purpose, degradation, dependencies, latent conditions and evidence.
Do not make "operator error" a terminal explanation or generate blame rankings.
| Jop Groeneweg / Tripod
| Organisational deficiencies can be investigated and addressed through structured
condition assessment and follow-through.
| Link observations to local conditions, organisational factors, accountable
interventions and verified learning. Treat factor categories as interpretive lenses.
| Ergonomics / human factors
| Human wellbeing and overall system performance are joint design concerns.
| Represent workload, task design, accessibility, environment, coordination and
distribution of burdens. Involve people doing the work in model revision.
| Leveson / STAMP and STPA
| Hazard scenarios can be analysed through control structure and unsafe control.
| Add control actions, constraints, feedback and process-model assumptions as a
distinct view. Do not flatten control flaws into failed hardware components.
| Hollnagel / FRAM
| Functional couplings and variability provide a way to examine everyday work.
| Represent work functions and their couplings as a future model/view; investigate
successful adaptation as well as unwanted outcomes.
| Simon; Kahneman
| Decision-making is bounded; judgement requires attention to how problems are framed.
| Make alternatives, dissent, reference classes, uncertainty and information costs
visible. Reduce decision burden instead of adding a mandatory field for every theory.
|===

The application consequences above are our synthesis, not claims that the named
authors endorsed this product or that their methods are interchangeable.

== Questions to answer with practitioners

. What decision is being made, by whom, for whom, over what horizon?
. How does work actually proceed, including adaptations that make it succeed?
. Where could control be lost, what follows, and which dependencies defeat several
  apparent defences at once?
. What supports each claim, what contradicts it, how old is the evidence, and what
  observation would change the decision?
. Who receives benefits and burdens? Which constraints cannot be traded away?
. What changed after an intervention, how will its effects be checked, and what
  reopens an apparently completed learning action?

== First evaluable hypothesis

An evidence-linked bowtie with explicit uncertainty and review triggers should
help a team identify unsupported critical-control claims and shared dependencies
more reliably than its existing static assessment, without excessive extra work.

Evaluate this on synthetic or consented cases before operational use. Compare
the existing process and prototype using counterbalanced tasks. Predefine seeded
missing evidence, a shared dependency, stale verification and a misleading
"within appetite" claim. Measure detection, appropriate abstention, explanation
traceability, review-trigger detection, task time and experienced workload.
Use independent domain review for the reference assessment, record disagreement,
and retain negative results. The sample size and success thresholds need a study
design; they are not invented here. NASA-TLX is a candidate workload instrument,
not a direct accident-risk measure.
